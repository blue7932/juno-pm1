## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno creates a brief spec for each recommended priority item in the top ten
- **Value proposition:** Draft a ticket for each recommended fix in the top ten and include a brief that contains a full summary of the problem, quotes and links to supporting documentation in the report,  one or more possible solutions, technical specs for each, a Claude Design prototype wherever possible.  Also pull a Clarity video or two to show examples of actual users experiencing the problem or at the moment of opportunity. Estimate impact on revenue, costs, productivity, or mission.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy (deal-breaker) | Did the AI correctly summarize the problem the user is having? | Yes / No | The AI adds context that the user didn't provide. The AI gets the problem wrong. | n/a | The user's problem is correctly interpreted and summarized. |
| Supporting quotes (deal-breaker) | Did the AI correctly supply actual quotes from users to back up each problem? | Yes / No | The AI didn't provide a quote, hallucinated a quote, misattributed a quote, or there's a mismatch between the quote and the problem the quote is supporting. | n/a | There's at least one quote for each problem. The quote is transcribed correctly, attributed to the right customer, and goes with the problem. |
| Clarity videos | Are there Clarity videos and do they show the right problem? | 1 to 5 | There is no Clarity video supplied. | There's a Clarity video that partially shows the problem. | There's at least one Clarity video that shows the entire user flow and clearly illustrates the exact problem the user specified. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** Score 5 answers per week
- **Who scores them with you:** Me and a junior PM
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** If we disagree on a score, we will manually review it together, discuss, and adjust the rubric to be more clear if necessary. I will have final say on the score in question.

## Pass bar

A yes or a score above 4 passes

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Accuracy: any "no"
- Supporting quotes: any "no"
- Anything hallucinated
